# Presentation path: one case from vignette to knowledge base

A short, invented case for the demo: a new mother asks whether she has ADHD. Her post shows a
lifelong attentional pattern, but sleep loss and low mood could explain the recent problems just as
well.

Copy each grey box as it is. The answers contain **deliberate mistakes** for the proofreading to fix;
they are listed after step 2.

---

## 1. Create the vignette (admin)

**Vignettes → New vignette.** Code `V900`, Where it was written: **Forum post**, Population `adult`.

**Patient summary**

```text
A 34-year-old mother of a 7-month-old, recently back at work, asks whether she has ADHD.
```

**Patient text** (128 words)

```text
Is this ADHD or just new mum brain?

I'm 34 and my daughter is 7 months old. Since going back to work I'm falling apart. I lose my keys every morning, I've missed two appointments and I read the same email four times without taking it in.

Part of it isn't new. Every school report said "bright but easily distracted". Before the baby I had lists and alarms for everything and my partner did all the bills. It worked. Now it doesn't.

But she still wakes every two hours, and I cry most days and feel like a terrible mum.

My brother was diagnosed at 30. My GP says it's normal with a new baby and to give it time.

How did you know it wasn't just exhaustion?
```

**Q4 what-if**

```text
her mood had lifted and the baby slept through the night, but she was still losing things and missing appointments
```

**Save → Publish → Assign** to the demo expert.

---

## 2. Answer it (expert)

**Mark four passages, in this order**, and set each direction:

| # | Select | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `Every school report said "bright but easily distracted".` | Points toward ADHD traits |
| 2 | `Before the baby I had lists and alarms for everything and my partner did all the bills.` | Points toward ADHD traits |
| 3 | `she still wakes every two hours` | Points away from ADHD traits |
| 4 | `I cry most days and feel like a terrible mum.` | Points away from ADHD traits |

**Paste each answer**, then replace each `[n]`: delete it and click passage *n*, which puts its chip
at the cursor.

**Q1**

```text
[1] places inattention in childhood and at school, which teh diagnosis requires. [2] is the strongest line: she needed a lot of structure to cope, and it broke down when demands rose. I read [3] and [4] the other way, becuase sleep loss and low mood alone could explain the recent forgetfulness.
```

**Q2** (written as short notes on purpose)

```text
lost keys, missed appointments, rereading emails - fit sleep loss [3] or depression [4] as well as ADHD. key difference is the course. ADHD: lifelong, persists once sleep and mood recover.
```

**Q3**

```text
Collateral history from her mother and the actual school reports. Screening for postnatal depression, and thyroid function, as postpartum thyroiditis affects both mood and concentation. sdfkj wqerpo lkjhq.
```

**Pass the gate.**

**Q4.** Hypothesis:

```text
Possible lifelong inattentive ADHD, compensated until now, that can't yet be told apart from postnatal depression and sleep loss.
```

My hypothesis… **partly changes**, towards… **more ADHD-like**. Why?

```text
If the problems persist once mood and sleep recover, the main alternative explanations are gone. Their is still the childhood history to confirm, so it is not a complete change.
```

**Q5**

```text
A less experienced clinician might put everything down to the new baby, as her GP did, and miss the lifelong pattern in [1] and [2]. Another might diagnose ADHD because her brother has it. She need treatment for the low mood first.
```

**Q6:** **3**. Then **Submit for proofreading**.

### What the proofreading should do

| Answer | Written | Becomes |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | `teh`, `becuase` | the, because |
| Q2 | `lost`, `key` at sentence start | Lost, Key; the notes are written out as full sentences |
| Q3 | `concentation` | concentration |
| Q3 | `sdfkj wqerpo lkjhq.` | dropped as unreadable |
| Q4 why | `Their is` | There is |
| Q5 | `She need` | She needs |

## 3. Check and review (no input)

1. **Check the edited text** (expert): the original and the edited answer side by side, changes
   marked, with the passages each line is about. **Submit for review.**
2. **Review**: a colleague approves each answer.
3. **Decision** (admin): approve. The answers are cut into units and enter the knowledge base
   (**In the knowledge base**).
